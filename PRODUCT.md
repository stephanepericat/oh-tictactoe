# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clever, technically fluent players, primarily software engineers, who want a quick competitive break alone or with another person. They expect the interface to be immediately understandable, fast to operate, and polished enough to reward repeat play.

## Product Purpose

Oh Tic-Tac-Toe provides a compact, satisfying game of tic-tac-toe in single-player and real-time online modes. The initial release must make starting a game effortless, clearly communicate every turn and outcome, and support short repeat sessions. Success means a player can choose an AI difficulty or join a friend by room code and begin playing without instructions.

## Brand Personality

Clever, playful, delightful.

The product should have the clarity and restraint of NYT Games while adding a more technical, lightly witty personality suited to software engineers.

## Anti-references

- Neon arcade and cyberpunk interfaces
- Casino styling, flashing rewards, or manipulative engagement patterns
- Childish toy aesthetics
- Sterile enterprise dashboard styling
- Decorative gradients, glassmorphism, or novelty controls that obscure standard interaction

## Design Principles

1. Make the next move obvious. A player should always know the mode, active turn, available action, and result.
2. Reward attention with wit. Delight should appear in concise copy, satisfying state changes, and small details, not visual noise.
3. Keep competition honest. Difficulty, connection state, room membership, score, and rematch state must be explicit.
4. Prefer familiar controls. Standard buttons, segmented choices, room codes, and keyboard behavior should disappear into the task.
5. Preserve the quick-game rhythm. Starting, replaying, and switching modes should take seconds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, screen-reader status announcements, and game states that do not rely on color alone.
